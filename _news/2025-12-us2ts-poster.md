I presented our poster **Kansas Fish Ontology** at the [5th U.S. Semantic Technologies Symposium (US2TS)](https://us2ts.org/), held December 8–9, 2025 at Wright State University in Dayton, Ohio. This is joint work with Martha Mather, Hande Küçük McGinty and Pascal Hitzler.

The Kansas Fish Ontology (KFO) brings together ecological, taxonomic and environmental data on Kansas fish species to support conservation. We built it with the KNARM methodology, stored it in GraphDB, and added the **Fish Ontology Visualizer**, a web interface for exploring fish-habitat relationships. [Read the abstract (PDF)](/assets/pdf/Kansas_Fish_Ontology_US2TS2025_abstract.pdf).
