My first-author paper **Hidden Neuron Activation Analysis on Labeled Text Data** was accepted at the **13th Knowledge Capture Conference (K-CAP 2025)**, and I presented it at the conference. The paper extends concept-induction neuron analysis from image models to text classifiers. The code is released as [text_hna](https://github.com/avishek-018/text_hna). [Read the paper (PDF)](https://daselab.cs.ksu.edu/uploads/3731443_3771369_feec3e7285.pdf).
