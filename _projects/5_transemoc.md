A toolkit for training, validating and predicting textual emotion with transformer models, with pretrained weights for Bengali. It packages the model from my NAACL 2021 Student Research Workshop paper for reuse.

**Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/avishek-018/TransEmoC) · [Code Ocean capsule](https://codeocean.com/capsule/6765604/tree/v1) · **Tools:** Python, TensorFlow, BERT.
