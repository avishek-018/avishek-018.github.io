A concept-induction pipeline that explains how a deep text classifier makes its decisions. It labels hidden neurons with concepts drawn from a knowledge graph built over the labeled text.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/publication_preview/kcap2025_hna.jpg" title="text_hna overview" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

This is the companion toolkit to the K-CAP 2025 paper *Hidden Neuron Activation Analysis on Labeled Text Data* ([PDF](https://daselab.cs.ksu.edu/uploads/3731443_3771369_feec3e7285.pdf)).

**Code:** [github.com/avishek-018/text_hna](https://github.com/avishek-018/text_hna) · **Tools:** Python, TensorFlow, DL-Learner.
