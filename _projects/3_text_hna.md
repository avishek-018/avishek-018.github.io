A concept-induction pipeline that explains how a deep text classifier makes its decisions. It labels hidden neurons with concepts drawn from a knowledge graph built over the labeled text.

This is the companion toolkit to the K-CAP 2025 paper _Hidden Neuron Activation Analysis on Labeled Text Data_.

**Code:** [github.com/avishek-018/text_hna](https://github.com/avishek-018/text_hna) · **Tools:** Python, TensorFlow, DL-Learner.
