## Research

**Graduate Research Assistant**, Data Semantics Lab, Kansas State University  
_July 2024 – present · Manhattan, KS · Advisor: Dr. Pascal Hitzler_

- Develop neurosymbolic interpretability methods that label what individual hidden neurons in deep networks detect, using concept induction (DL-Learner) over OWL knowledge graphs.
- Extended this neuron analysis from image models to text classifiers (first-author paper at K-CAP 2025) and released the [text_hna]({{ '/projects/3_text_hna/' | relative_url }}) toolkit.
- Analyze how CNNs represent contextual image patches in agricultural datasets to expose context bias (preprint, 2026).
- Investigating the internal representations of open-weight LLMs (Gemma) by labeling sparse-autoencoder features with human-readable concepts (ongoing).
- Collaborated with the Division of Biology on the [Kansas Fish Ontology]({{ '/projects/2b_kansas_fish_ontology/' | relative_url }}) (poster at US2TS 2025), and with the Department of Biological and Agricultural Engineering on predicting industrial sprayer boom movement.

**Research Coordinator**, CUET NLP Lab, Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology  
_June 2021 – June 2024 · Chattogram, Bangladesh_

- Supervised 2 and co-supervised 25 undergraduate researchers in NLP, multimodal learning and LLMs, producing about 20 peer-reviewed papers at venues including EACL, EMNLP workshops and ICCIT.
- Fine-tuned transformer models (BERT, MuRIL, multilingual masked language models) for emotion, sentiment, hate-speech and fake-news detection in Bengali, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam.
