## Before K-State

I was a Lecturer in Computer Science & Engineering at [CUET](https://www.cuet.ac.bd/) and the research coordinator of the CUET NLP Lab, where I supervised more than 25 undergraduate researchers working on low-resource NLP and multimodal learning. In industry, I built the Bangla OCR system used in the Government of Bangladesh's national OCR service at Apurba Technologies, and I designed AI skill assessments for Workera.

## At a glance

- **Education:** Ph.D. in Computer Science, Kansas State University (2024–present, expected Summer 2028). M.Sc. (2025) and B.Sc. (2021, with Honors, ranked 4th of 120) in CSE, Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology.
- **Research:** Graduate Research Assistant, DaSe Lab, Kansas State University (2024–present). Research Coordinator, CUET NLP Lab (2021–2024).
- **Teaching:** Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, CUET (2022–2024).
- **Industry:** Assessment Developer at Workera (2023–2024). Machine Learning Engineer at Apurba Technologies (2021–2022).
- **Grants:** co-writer on seven proposals to NSF, DOE, USDA-NIFA and Amazon.
- **Service:** subreviewer for the World Conference on eXplainable AI (xAI 2026). Reviewer for IEEE Access and IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems. Secretary of CS-GSA at K-State. Former Vice-Chair of IEEE Young Professionals, Bangladesh Section.
- **Outreach:** volunteer robotics instructor for about 100 rural school students in Bangladesh (2018–2019).
