Avishek Das

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science · Kansas State University · Data Semantics Lab

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Kansas State University, advised by Dr. Pascal Hitzler in the Data Semantics Lab. My research asks a simple question: what has a neural network actually learned? I build neurosymbolic methods that label the hidden units of deep networks with human-readable concepts, using concept induction over OWL knowledge graphs [1–2] 1Toward a Neurosymbolic Understanding of Hidden Neuron ActivationsNeurosymbolic Artificial Intelligence 2026 2Neurosymbolic Hidden Neuron Analysis in Convolutional Neural NetworksElsevier book chapter 2026 , and extend them from images to text, audio and contextual bias [3–5] 3Hidden Neuron Activation Analysis on Labeled Text DataK-CAP 2025 4Bridging Neural Representations and Symbolic Semantics in Audio ClassificationarXiv 2026 5Understanding Contextual Bias in Agricultural CNNs Through Hidden-Neuron Concept InductionarXiv 2026 . I am now applying these ideas to sparse-autoencoder features inside open-weight LLMs, and I also build knowledge-graph systems for LLM agents [6–7] 6ML Knowledge Hub: agentic hybrid GraphRAG with an MCP serverProject · live demo 7Reasoning in Large Language Models: RAG and BeyondElsevier book chapter 2026 .
